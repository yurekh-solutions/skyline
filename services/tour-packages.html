Before you start

Documents we will ask for.

Vehicles, drivers, berths and itineraries confirmed in writing with a fixed rate, so nothing is negotiated on the day of the trip.

Checklist — Tour Packages

  • Traveller names as per ID proof
  • Passport and visa for international tours
  • Travel dates and number of adults and children
  • Preferred hotel category and room configuration
  • Special requests — diet, medical, wheelchair, baby cot
  • Emergency contact for the group lead

Photographs on WhatsApp are enough for us to verify the set. Originals are matched at filing, or at the department where they demand your presence.

Asked most often

Three quick answers on Tour Packages.

How long does Tour Packages take?

Itinerary and quote in 24–48 hours; booking once the advance is received.

What documents are needed for Tour Packages?

Traveller names as per ID proof; Passport and visa for international tours; Travel dates and number of adults and children; Preferred hotel category and room configuration — the full checklist is above, and we confirm it against your case before any fee is paid.

Can you handle Tour Packages if I am outside Mumbai?

Yes. Documents are collected over WhatsApp or email, the filing is done on the relevant portal, and originals travel by courier. Where physical verification is required, it happens at the office nearest to you.
