Asked most often

Three quick answers on Term Insurance.

How long does Term Insurance take?

Policy issued in 7–12 days after medical underwriting.

What documents are needed for Term Insurance?

Filled proposal form; PAN and Aadhaar; Address proof and photographs; Income proof for the sum assured applied — the full checklist is above, and we confirm it against your case before any fee is paid.

Can you handle Term Insurance if I am outside Mumbai?

Yes. Documents are collected over WhatsApp or email, the filing is done on the relevant portal, and originals travel by courier. Where physical verification is required, it happens at the office nearest to you.
