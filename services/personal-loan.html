Before you start

Documents we will ask for.

One file, several lenders, compared on rate and net disbursal — not on a promise. We prepare the application the way credit teams like to read it.

Checklist — Personal Loan

  • PAN and Aadhaar
  • Salary slips for the last 3 months
  • Form 16 or ITR for the last 2 years
  • Salary account statement for the last 6 months
  • Address proof and employer ID card
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Credit report consent for verification

Photographs on WhatsApp are enough for us to verify the set. Originals are matched at filing, or at the department where they demand your presence.

03

Asked most often

Three quick answers on Personal Loan.

How long does Personal Loan take?

In-principle approval in 24–72 hours; disbursal in 3–7 days.

What documents are needed for Personal Loan?

PAN and Aadhaar; Salary slips for the last 3 months; Form 16 or ITR for the last 2 years; Salary account statement for the last 6 months — the full checklist is above, and we confirm it against your case before any fee is paid.

Can you handle Personal Loan if I am outside Mumbai?

Yes. Documents are collected over WhatsApp or email, the filing is done on the relevant portal, and originals travel by courier. Where physical verification is required, it happens at the office nearest to you.
