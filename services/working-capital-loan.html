Before you start

Documents we will ask for.

One file, several lenders, compared on rate and net disbursal — not on a promise. We prepare the application the way credit teams like to read it.

Checklist — Working Capital Loan

  • PAN and GST certificate
  • Audited financials for the last 2 years
  • Stock and book-debts statements
  • Current account statement for the last 12 months
  • Sales bills, order book and supplier details
  • Projected turnover and fund requirement
  • KYC of proprietors, partners or directors
  • Hypothecation and security documents

Photographs on WhatsApp are enough for us to verify the set. Originals are matched at filing, or at the department where they demand your presence.

How long does Working Capital Loan take?

What documents are needed for Working Capital Loan?

PAN and GST certificate; Audited financials for the last 2 years; Stock and book-debts statements; Current account statement for the last 12 months — the full checklist is above, and we confirm it against your case before any fee is paid.

Can you handle Working Capital Loan if I am outside Mumbai?

Yes. Documents are collected over WhatsApp or email, the filing is done on the relevant portal, and originals travel by courier. Where physical verification is required, it happens at the office nearest to you.
