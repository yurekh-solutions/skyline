01

Before you start

Documents we will ask for.

Government work is won or lost on the paperwork. We read the application the way the officer will, fill what can be filled online and stand at the counter for the rest.

Checklist — ITR Filing

  • PAN and Aadhaar
  • Form 16 from every employer
  • Bank statement and interest certificates
  • Investment proofs under 80C, 80D, 80G
  • Capital-gains and mutual-fund statements
  • Form 26AS and AIS / ITR summary download
  • Previous year ITR-V
  • Digital signature, where audit applies

Photographs on WhatsApp are enough for us to verify the set. Originals are matched at filing, or at the department where they demand your presence.

03

Asked most often

Three quick answers on ITR Filing.

How long does ITR Filing take?

Return filed in 1–2 days once documents are ready; acknowledgement instantly on e-verification.

What documents are needed for ITR Filing?

PAN and Aadhaar; Form 16 from every employer; Bank statement and interest certificates; Investment proofs under 80C, 80D, 80G — the full checklist is above, and we confirm it against your case before any fee is paid.

Can you handle ITR Filing if I am outside Mumbai?

Yes. Documents are collected over WhatsApp or email, the filing is done on the relevant portal, and originals travel by courier. Where physical verification is required, it happens at the office nearest to you.
