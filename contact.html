Visiting questions

Practical things people ask.

Where exactly is the office in Santacruz East?

Gate No 1, Hanuman Tekdi, near Santacruz East Metro Station, Mumbai 400055. Come out of Gate 1, and the tekdi is right there — we are on the lane by it. Use the map link above for turn-by-turn.

What are the opening hours?

Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 20:00 IST. Sunday is closed for walk-ins; WhatsApp messages sent on Sunday are answered Monday morning.

Is there parking nearby?

Two-wheeler parking is available on the lane by Gate No 1. For cars, the nearest paid parking is on the metro station side, about a three-minute walk.

Can I walk in without an appointment?

Yes, during working hours. If you want a specific person for a loan or a company matter, call 8271895469 first — one of the two owners is usually at the desk.

Do I have to come at all?

Usually not. Most work runs on WhatsApp photos of documents plus one OTP from you. You travel only when a department, bank or insurer needs biometrics or an original verification.
