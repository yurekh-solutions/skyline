04

Common questions

Asked before we file, and after.

Do I have to visit your office in person?

Usually no. Most work starts on a call or WhatsApp with photographs of your documents. You come only when a department, bank or insurer requires biometrics, signatory presence or an original verification. We are at Gate No 1, Hanuman Tekdi, near Santacruz East Metro Station.

When do I pay?

Government and registration fees are stated before filing and paid against a receipt. Our service charge is agreed upfront in writing on WhatsApp or email — nothing is added after the work starts.

Can you guarantee how fast the government will approve my file?

No honest consultant can. We tell you the realistic range for that department, file a complete application so it is not sent back for objections, and follow up until it moves.

What if my application is rejected or sent back?

We read the objection, tell you exactly what is missing and refile or respond within the notice period. Responding to an objection is included in the original scope for the same application.

Do you handle clients outside Mumbai?

Yes. Documents are collected over WhatsApp or email, filings are done on the relevant portal, and originals travel by courier. Physical verification, where required, happens at the office nearest to you.

How do I track progress after filing?

Three updates are guaranteed: when the checklist is confirmed, when the application is filed with the acknowledgement number, and when the certificate, sanction letter or ticket is issued. Ask any day in between and we tell you the current stage.

Are my documents and numbers safe with you?

They are used for the filing you engaged us for and nothing else. Originals are returned the same sitting where possible, and access is limited to the two people handling your file.

Which documents should I keep ready before the first call?

PAN, Aadhaar, one address proof, the latest ITR or Form 16 if income is involved, and a passport-size photograph. The exact set for your job is on each service page.
